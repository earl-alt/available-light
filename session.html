How it works

A two-hour sample session

1 · Talk

Walk through the boards together. You tick off what's on the list and what's off-limits.

2 · Sign

Each of you signs a release and shows a photo ID. The ID copy is kept privately on file.

3 · Style

Nails, lips, satin, haze and light. About 30 minutes.

4 · Shoot

About 60 minutes in a rented studio, not at home. Anyone can stop at any time.

5 · Review

You see the picks first. Anything you reject gets deleted.

6 · Share

Your percentage of sales from the samples is paid to you monthly, with a simple statement.
