The character

"Hi, I'm Shannon McGhee. Wanna play with me?"

Shannon is loud, funny, curvy and completely in charge. She laughs at her own jokes and she runs the room; she isn't there to be looked at. The energy is a Sofía Vergara–style bombshell, played by our own people. We don't use anyone's real face or name.

Oxblood and cherry lacquer, big hair, gold jewelry, and satin with lace.
